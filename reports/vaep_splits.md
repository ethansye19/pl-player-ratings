# VAEP: train/score splits, model settings and model quality

Generated by `python -m src.vaep.report` on 2026-10-31 from the outputs of `python -m src.vaep.train` (StatsBomb Open Data, Premier League 2015/16: 380 matches, 757,641 SPADL actions). Training run time: 4.4 min (feature step 31 s).

## Splits

Two kinds of split, both recorded with their full match-id lists in `data/processed/vaep_splits.json` (`{"holdout": {"train", "score"}, "folds": [{"fold_id", "train", "score"}, ...]}`):

- **Holdout** (chronological): train on matchweeks 1-19 (190 matches), score matchweeks 20-38 (190 matches). Output: `raw_vaep_holdout.parquet`.
- **5-fold cross-fitting**: a seeded shuffle (seed 20152016) of all 380 matches into 5 folds of 76 matches. The model of fold *k* is trained on the other folds and scores only fold *k*, so every match is scored exactly once by a model that never saw it. Outputs: `action_values.parquet` (with `fold_id`) and `raw_vaep.parquet`.
- Inside every split, a seeded 15% of the *training* games is set aside for early stopping (whole games, never rows, because consecutive game states share labels); the rest fits the trees. Score games are never used for fitting or round selection.

## Model settings

- `socceraction 1.4.2` `VAEP(nb_prev_actions=3)` with the default feature transformers (`xfns_default`: action type, result, type x result and body-part one-hots, time, start/end location, polar coordinates, movement, possession-team flags, time and space deltas, score line) -> 568 feature columns per game state (the action and its 2 predecessors), pitch flipped so the acting team attacks left to right.
- Labels: `scores` / `concedes` = the acting team scores / concedes within the next 10 actions (socceraction's defaults). Values: `offensive_value` = change in P(scores), `defensive_value` = -change in P(concedes), `vaep_value` = their sum (`socceraction.vaep.formula`).
- Learner: `lightgbm 4.5.0` `LGBMClassifier`, one per label, fitted through `VAEP.fit(learner="lightgbm")` with early stopping on binary log loss (patience 30 rounds) on the validation games. socceraction's own `val_size` option is not used: it samples validation rows (not games) and relies on the `early_stopping_rounds` fit argument that lightgbm 4 removed.
- Determinism: `SEED = 20152016` seeds the fold shuffle, the validation-game draw, socceraction's row shuffle (`np.random.seed`) and lightgbm (`random_state`, `deterministic=True`, `force_row_wise=True`). Verified over four runs of the CLI (two computing the features, two loading the cache): `action_values.parquet`, `raw_vaep.parquet`, `raw_vaep_holdout.parquet`, `vaep_splits.json` and the feature/label caches were byte-identical every time, with identical best iterations and quality metrics. The model pickles are byte-identical between runs that take the same feature path; between the two paths their bytes differ only through pickle's string memoisation (the unpickled models re-pickle identically), not in content.
- Features are cast to float32 for fitting; scoring uses the float64 features computed on the fly from the match's actions, exactly as `src.vaep.rate.rate_match` does.
