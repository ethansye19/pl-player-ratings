# VAEP: train/score splits, model settings and model quality

Generated by `python -m src.vaep.report` on 2026-11-02 from the outputs of `python -m src.vaep.train --labels xg` (StatsBomb Open Data, Premier League 2015/16: 380 matches, 757,641 SPADL actions). Training run time: 3.6 min (feature step 0 s, label step 1 s).

Framework: VAEP (Decroos, Bransen, Van Haaren and Davis, "Actions Speak Louder than Goals: Valuing Player Actions in Soccer", KDD 2019), as implemented in `socceraction`. Shots are valued by chance quality: the labels are built from StatsBomb expected goals instead of the shot outcome (section *Shots valued by expected goals* below), and since 2026-11-02 two scoring rules turn the probabilities into values without retraining (section *Scoring rules (option 3)*; the action values were re-scored with `python -m src.vaep.score`). VAEP is the experimental rating; the baseline is the headline.

## Splits

Two kinds of split, both recorded with their full match-id lists in `data/processed/vaep_splits.json` (`{"holdout": {"train", "score"}, "folds": [{"fold_id", "train", "score"}, ...]}`):

- **Holdout** (chronological): train on matchweeks 1-19 (190 matches), score matchweeks 20-38 (190 matches). Output: `raw_vaep_holdout.parquet`.
- **5-fold cross-fitting**: a seeded shuffle (seed 20152016) of all 380 matches into 5 folds of 76 matches. The model of fold *k* is trained on the other folds and scores only fold *k*, so every match is scored exactly once by a model that never saw it. Outputs: `action_values.parquet` (with `fold_id`) and `raw_vaep.parquet`.
- Inside every split, a seeded 15% of the *training* games is set aside for early stopping (whole games, never rows, because consecutive game states share labels); the rest fits the trees. Score games are never used for fitting or round selection.

The splits are identical to the frozen pre-change `before/vaep_splits.json`.

## Model settings

- `socceraction 1.4.2` `VAEP(nb_prev_actions=3)` with the default feature transformers (`xfns_default`: action type, result, type x result and body-part one-hots, time, start/end location, polar coordinates, movement, possession-team flags, time and space deltas, score line) -> 568 feature columns per game state (the action and its 2 predecessors), pitch flipped so the acting team attacks left to right. The features and the game-state construction are unchanged from the outcome-label run.
- Labels (`src/vaep/labels.py`): `scores` / `concedes` for the window of the action and the next 9 actions -- soft xG-based labels (probability that the acting team scores / concedes from the chances in the window, see the next section). Values: `offensive_value` = change in P(scores), `defensive_value` = -change in P(concedes), `vaep_value` = their sum, from socceraction's formula plus the two option-3 scoring rules in `src/vaep/value.py` (section *Scoring rules (option 3)*).
- Learner: `lightgbm 4.5.0` `lightgbm.LGBMRegressor(objective='cross_entropy')`, one per label, through `src.vaep.model.SoftLabelVAEP` (a `socceraction.vaep.VAEP` subclass that replaces only fit/predict: socceraction's `VAEP.fit` builds `LGBMClassifier`s, which reject targets strictly between 0 and 1). Early stopping on the validation games' cross-entropy (patience 30 rounds); the tree parameters below are those of the outcome-label run.
- Determinism: `SEED = 20152016` seeds the fold shuffle, the validation-game draw and lightgbm (`random_state`, `deterministic=True`, `force_row_wise=True`); the row order is the action order, so a rerun reproduces every value.
- Features are cast to float32 for fitting; scoring uses the float64 features computed on the fly from the match's actions, exactly as `src.vaep.rate.rate_match` does (train.py builds `raw_vaep*.parquet` through that same function).

## Shots valued by expected goals

**Why.** With outcome labels (validation finding B) VAEP is a goals-and-misses detector for attackers: a goal is worth about +0.8, every unconverted shot is charged the whole chance it consumed, a converted penalty is worth little to the taker and a missed one costs about -0.75. The standard remedy in the VAEP literature for this outcome sensitivity is to replace the binary goal labels by expected-goals-based labels, so that a shot's offensive value reflects the quality of the chance rather than whether it went in.

**Label formula.** For game state *i* and the window *W(i)* of the action and the next 9 actions (socceraction's window and team logic, unchanged):

    scores(i)   = 1 - prod_{j in W(i)} (1 - g_j)   over shots j by the acting team; an own goal by the opponent counts g = 1
    concedes(i) = 1 - prod_{j in W(i)} (1 - g_j)   over shots j by the opponent;    an own goal by the acting team counts g = 1

where `g_j` is the StatsBomb xG of shot *j*, i.e. the probability that at least one chance in the window is converted when shots are independent. With `g_j` replaced by the outcome (1 for a goal, 0 otherwise) the formula is exactly socceraction's OR of goal flags, and a unit test checks that equivalence. Positions past the last action of the game contribute nothing (socceraction repeats the last action there, which is harmless for an OR but would multiply a final chance into the product several times). The models are then regressed on these soft labels with lightgbm's `cross_entropy` objective, and the values come from socceraction's formula applied to the predicted probabilities, with the two option-3 scoring rules on top (below).

**Join to StatsBomb xG and coverage.** SPADL shot actions (`shot`, `shot_penalty`, `shot_freekick`) are joined to the StatsBomb event through `original_event_id` = events `id`, taking `shot_statsbomb_xg`: 9,908 shot actions, 0 without an xG value (those would fall back to the binary outcome). Every penalty carries StatsBomb's constant penalty xG (0.7835) whatever happened to it. 38 own goals have no xG and count as a goal (1.0) for the team that benefits and as a goal conceded for the other. Note: in the SPADL conversion an own goal is a `bad_touch` with result `owngoal`, and socceraction's outcome labels only recognise own goals on shot-type actions, so the previous run did not see them; the xG labels do (the only place where the two label definitions differ beyond the xG substitution).

**Consequences for who gets the credit.** VAEP values an action by the change in P(scores) / P(concedes) from the previous game state, so with xG labels the chance is credited to the actions that create it and the shot itself is worth only the difference between its state and the one before it: an open-play goal +0.122 on average and a shot that is not scored +0.022. Because a shot's state keeps its xG whatever the outcome, the drop back to a low probability happens at the *next* action within 10 seconds (the formula zeroes the previous state after a longer gap). Under option 3 only a keeper save collects that drop: a save is worth +0.085 on average (outcome labels: -0.004); any other first action after an unscored shot is measured against the chance left after the miss, so a clearance straight after a shot is worth +0.027 on average. Penalties: the label of a penalty state is its constant xG (0.7835) for scored and missed kicks alike, but the models under-predict the 91 penalty states (too few to be isolated from other shots -- a holdout experiment with `min_child_samples=20` instead of 100 moved the mean prediction only from 0.57 to 0.62 against a label of 0.78 -- and the result one-hot pulls missed kicks towards failed shots), which made a converted penalty worth about -0.11 to the taker and a miss about -0.30. Option 3 sets the P(scores) of a penalty state to the penalty's xG, so against socceraction's fixed 0.792 pre-kick probability the kick is now worth -0.009 when scored and -0.009 when missed: neutral to the taker whatever happens. The chance's value lands where it was won: the foul that concedes the penalty is worth -0.459 to the fouler (n=91).

## Scoring rules (option 3)

Decision 2026-11-02, after the re-validation (`reports/validation.md`, sections 6 and 8). The fold models, features, labels and splits are unchanged; only the step from the predicted probabilities to the action values is (`src/vaep/value.py`, applied by `SoftLabelVAEP.rate`):

1. **Penalties.** The P(scores) of every `shot_penalty` state is set to that penalty's StatsBomb xG (0.7835), so the kick is worth xG - 0.792453 to the taker, about -0.01, whether it is scored or missed.
2. **After an unscored shot.** The next action is measured against the shot state's P(scores) with the shot's own chance taken out, `1 - (1 - p) / (1 - xG)` clipped to `[0, p]`, instead of against the whole state; a `keeper_save` keeps the full drop, because the save is what stopped the chance. Shots without xG are left as they are.

Both rules use only the match's own actions, probabilities and shot xG (`rate_match` reads that match's shots from `events.parquet`), so rating a match on its own still reproduces the season output; a test checks it.
